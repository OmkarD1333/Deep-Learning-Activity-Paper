Weighted segmentation loss

Each pixel receives class-balance weight plus a boundary term that emphasizes narrow gaps between touching cells.

w(x) = w_c(x) + w₀ exp(-(d₁+d₂)² / 2σ²)
w₀ = 10, σ = 5 px
